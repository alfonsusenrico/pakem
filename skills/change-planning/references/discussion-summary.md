# Discussion Summary

Status: Active
Applies to: brainstorms and design discussions
Last reviewed: 2026-10-07
Review by: 2027-04-07
Sources: owner Working Standard Profile, section 3

Save as `research/notes/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`.

- **Problem**: the confirmed problem statement, in the owner's or client's terms.
- **Users and context**: who is affected and how they work today.
- **Constraints**: budget, deadline, maintainers after handover, hosting and operations,
  scale, data sensitivity (only those that matter).
- **Acceptance**: what must be true for the outcome to count as done.
- **Options considered**: two or three, each with trade-offs and evidence or sources.
- **Recommendation and decision**: the agent's recommendation and the owner's choice,
  with the reason.
- **Open questions**: what is still unknown, and who answers it.
- **Next step**: phased plan, OpenSpec proposal, prototype, or further research.
