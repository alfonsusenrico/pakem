# Choosing a Design Direction

Status: Active
Applies to: new products, new surfaces, major redesigns
Last reviewed: 2026-10-07
Review by: 2027-04-07
Sources: owner practice (portfolio prototypes and review routes; dashboard layout selector; workbench design lab), approved 2026-10-07

1. Gather references: products the owner names, the existing system, and the
   content that must be shown. Write a short brief (audience, surface, tone, must-keep).
2. Build two to four working alternatives with real (sanitized) content, either as
   unlinked no-index review routes in the app or under `research/prototypes/<topic>/`.
   Vary one dimension at a time when possible (layout, palette, type pairing).
3. Compare in the browser at phone and desktop widths with the owner. Keep earlier
   previews available for side-by-side comparison.
4. Record the decision in `research/notes/` and the project's design notes: the
   chosen option, rejected options with the owner's reasons, and what must not change.
5. Implement through an OpenSpec change. After acceptance: refine, don't restyle.
   New identities are proposed only when the owner asks.
6. Remove review routes before release or keep them unlinked and no-index, as the
   project decides.
